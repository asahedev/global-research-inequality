# Global Research Inequality: Who Gets to Do Science?

An exploratory data analysis of the global distribution of R&D researchers,
using data from UNESCO and the World Bank covering 144 countries from 1996 to 2023.

## Motivation

Science thrives on diverse perspectives — but access to research careers is far
from equal across the world. This project visualizes that inequality and explores
which regions and countries are most underrepresented in the global research workforce.

## Contents

- `global_research_inequality.ipynb` — analysis notebook

## Visualizations

- **Choropleth world map** — most recent researcher density per country
- **Regional trends line chart** — how research capacity has evolved by world region since 1996
- **Bar chart** — the 20 most underrepresented countries in the dataset

## Data Source

UNESCO UIS Stat Bulk Data Download Service, via World Bank (2026),
processed by Our World in Data.
Retrieved from: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/researchers-in-rd-per-million-people
